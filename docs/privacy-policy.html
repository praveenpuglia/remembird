Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Remembird app (hereby referred to as "Application") for Android devices, created by Praveen Puglia (hereby referred to as "Service Provider"). The Application is free and is provided "AS IS".


The short version

Remembird reads the birthdays, anniversaries and other dates saved in your contacts and shows them to you. That information never leaves your phone. The Application has no internet access, no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and the Service Provider receives nothing from it.


Information the Application uses

With your permission (Android's "Contacts" permission, read only), the Application reads these details from the contacts already on your phone:

  • contact names and contact photos
  • dates saved on contacts, such as birthdays, anniversaries and custom dates, including their labels

These are read on your device, each time you open the Application, only to show you upcoming dates. The Application does not copy them into its own storage, does not keep a history, and does not include them in device backups.


No collection, no sharing

The Application does not request internet access, so it cannot send your contacts or any other information anywhere. The Service Provider does not collect, store, sell or share any personal or device information, and no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services are included.


Adding and editing dates

The Application never changes your contacts itself. When you add a date, it opens your phone's contacts app with the date filled in, and you choose whether to save it there. Changing or removing a date also happens in your contacts app. What your contacts app does with that information, for example syncing it to your Google account, is governed by that app's own settings and privacy policy.


Google Play

If you install the Application from Google Play, Google may collect information as described in Google's own privacy policy, for example to handle downloads and crash reports you choose to share with developers. This happens outside the Application.


Your choices

You can revoke the Contacts permission at any time in your phone's Settings, and the Application will stop reading your contacts. Uninstalling the Application removes it completely; since it stores nothing, nothing is left behind.


Children

The Application is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.


Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time, for example when the Application gains new features such as reminders. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
